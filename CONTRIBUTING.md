# Contributing

Thanks for helping map the world's QRM.

The easiest way to contribute will be from inside the **QRM Hunter** app: after you confirm a source, choose **Share to library**. The app fills in the entry, shows you exactly what will be uploaded, and opens the submission for you. This arrives in a future release.

## Manual submissions (once the format is published)

1. Fork this repo and add a folder `entries/<uuid>/` with `entry.json` and `waterfall.png`.
2. Open a pull request. CI checks the entry against the schema.
3. A maintainer reviews it before merging.

## What makes a good entry

- **The source is confirmed.** You found the device and the noise stopped when it was switched off or fixed. Entries for suspected sources go in an issue instead.
- **The screenshot is clear,** with the interference visible on the waterfall.
- **You record how you found it,** e.g. portable receiver, switching breakers off one at a time, near-field probe. This is what helps the next person.
- **Nothing private is included.** No addresses or exact coordinates, and no photos of people or homes.
